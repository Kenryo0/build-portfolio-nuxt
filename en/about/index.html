About Me

A producer who speaks developer. But what does that even mean?
Hello, I'm Adrien Le Deunff, a former web developer who is now transitioning into video game producing. I have always been fascinated by game creation and decided to embark on this new adventure while making the shift from technical to human-centric work.I have a deep understanding of the fusion between technical and artistic aspects, and I am convinced that the best way to create digital products is to combine these two aspects harmoniously.

Programmer Background

Before joining ENJMIN, I worked as a web developer from 2018 to 2024. These 6 years in the professional world were rich with lessons about work management, project management, and teamwork. These years gave me the opportunity to tackle diverse business challenges, first in IT service companies where we developed B2B and B2C websites for airports, public hospitals, as well as local businesses like a wine shop or a market gardener. Later, service provision led me to work for a banking player and the public transport sector. This wealth of business experience gave me a particular taste and attraction for understanding needs expressed by clients and translating them into technical challenges, thus proposing relevant solutions, whether on the visual or purely functional aspect.

My Team Philosophy

This diversity of experience has also taught me a lot about my approach to teamwork, having seen different types of organization, from small teams to services composed of several large teams. These latter structures led me to take on a lead developer responsibility and gradually to pilot small teams on task execution within a framework respecting collectively defined workflows. My team philosophy is rooted in empathy and problem-solving. I believe it all starts with understanding the 'why' – the needs and motivations of my team. I rely on research, participative methodologies (Poker planning) and anticipative methods (Planning, roadmap) to ensure that the solutions I build are truly effective.

What Drives Me

I am driven by the constant adaptation required within a team, to understand everyone's needs and constraints. It is a constant challenge to translate complex ideas into concrete and elegant solutions.

Outside of Work

When I'm not working on a project, you can find me in the forest reconnecting with nature. When I'm not wandering around, I immerse myself in universes I find fascinating, both in video games and reading. And when I'm doing none of that, I write scenarios that I have friends play out as role-playing game sessions.Thanks for stopping by. Feel free to explore my projects and/or get in touch!
